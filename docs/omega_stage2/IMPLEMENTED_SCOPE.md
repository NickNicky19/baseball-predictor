# Implemented scope and non-claims

## Implemented

- Canonical date and aware-UTC parsing for the new candidate namespace.
- Unconditional May 2026 date/path rejection before candidate readers open data.
- Positive feature schemas with units, counts, denominators, samples, source/receipt types, consumer allowlists, finite/range checks, and subset relations.
- Semantic receipt resolution that verifies manifest bytes, raw bytes, parser/protocol/source identity, chronology, projection hash, and relied-upon sports identity.
- Typed artifact manifests with ordered windows, valid timestamps, required evaluation date/horizon, complete hash fields, immutable content-addressed publication, and fail-closed research-only state.
- Hash-bound, predeclared, market-separated evaluation coverage and
  candidate-vs-comparator non-regression. Paired scoring consumers are limited
  to the returned exact common-gradeable keys.
- Frozen production source boundary verification.
- A display-only dashboard and staging/CI artifacts as separately documented.

## Not implemented

- No model fit or calibrated coefficient.
- No Omega probability runner connected to live or dashboard output.
- No projected-lineup, pitcher exposure, contact, weather, park, umpire, injury, run-context, or market-price feature effect.
- No outcome scoring or prospective adjudication.
- No executable price, order, fill, settlement, CLV, ROI, or betting authorization.
- No collector, schedule, ledger, AWS production, DNS, or live-model change.
- No May 2026 access.

Passing these contract tests means the isolated safety boundaries behave as declared. It does not mean the future model is accurate or qualified.
